The site

No cookies, no analytics, no scripts or fonts from other parties. We do not know who visits.

The waitlist

When you join the list we send your email address and your language choice to our mailbox through Formspree (Formspree Inc., United States), which keeps a copy of your submission in its own environment. The transfer outside the EU rests on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission. We use your address only to tell you when we are live; the legal basis is your request for that. We keep it until that message plus twelve months, share it with nobody else and never use it for newsletters.

Hosting

The site is a static page on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., United States). The host may keep technical data such as IP addresses briefly in server logs for security.

Your rights

You can always ask to see, correct or delete your data via info@harnashq.com. If you disagree with how we handle it, you can lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
